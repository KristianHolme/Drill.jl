## Breaking

- The default AD backend is now Enzyme: `init` and `solve` use `ad_type = AutoEnzyme()` instead of `AutoZygote()`.
  Load Enzyme (`using Enzyme`) before training, or pass `ad_type = AutoZygote()` and load Zygote.
- `sac_actor_loss` and `sac_critic_loss` take the actor and critic parameters and states as separate arguments:
  `sac_actor_loss(alg, model, actor_ps, actor_st, critic_ps, critic_st, data; rng)` and
  `sac_critic_loss(alg, model, critic_ps, critic_st, data)`. Both return the updated states of their own part only.

## Added

- `actor_features`, `critic_features` and `action_log_prob_from_features` for `ContinuousActorCriticModel`.

## Fixed

- SAC trains with plain `AutoEnzyme()`. Enzyme runtime activity is no longer needed.
